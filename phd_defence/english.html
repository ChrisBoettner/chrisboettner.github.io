🎓 The PhD Defence in the Netherlands

In the Netherlands, the PhD defence is more of a ceremony than anything else. The PhD candidate (me! :D) receives questions from a committee of opponents, generally experts from domains connected to the topic of my PhD.

The great thing about this defence is that it's completely public, so you all can watch me sweat for 45 minutes trying my best to answer these questions, and I would like you all to be there! Also there's a party afterwards.

📚 The PhD thesis

The PhD thesis around here is basically a collection of research articles that I have written over the course of the last four years, published, together with an introduction, conclusion, summary (written to be understandable for anyone!), acknowledgements (where you might find yourself), and a short biography. It's also customary to design a pretty cover and print a number physical copies that can be distributed to researchers, faculty member, friends and family. The title of my thesis is:

🏛️ About Groningen

Groningen is a university town with a population of about 200,000 people. It's a lovely city, and I can't wait to show it to you! Also everyone speaks perfect English around here.


🚗 Getting to Groningen

Groningen is easily accessible by train from Amsterdam (2.5 hours) and Berlin (5-6 hours). A cheaper option from Berlin is Flixbus, which has a direct connection to Groningen (8 hours).


🏨 Where to Stay

Everything within the central canal ring (see picture below) is within walking distance - this is also where the defence and party will be! So getting a place there is nice, but maybe a bit pricey. Areas outside the canals are also quite close, as Groningen is quite small. There are quite a few hotels, hostels and Airbnbs around. Just text me if you have any questions; I'm happy to help arrange accommodation. And if all options are too expensive, I can try to find you a friendly couch if needed.


🚴 Getting Around

Groningen is famously bike-friendly - cycling is the best way to get around, and there are many bike rentals. In fact, cars are mostly banned in the city centre, and like I said it's quite walkable anyway. There are also buses around, which should be able to get you anywhere you need to go. Be aware they don't take cash, though!

👯‍♂️ My Paranymphs

In the Dutch PhD system, it is tradition that the PhD candidate is supported by two of their most trusted friends during the defence ceremony. These two paranymphs play a mostly ceremonial role nowadays, but they still help planning the defence, reception and party.

I couldn't be happier to have my paranymphs, Célestin and Antonio, at my side during the final steps of my degree. Thank you for being such great friends, for helping and supporting me throughout the PhD, and for taking on this role!
